ADLC Spec Intake

Paste meeting notes, a chat transcript or a ticket dump. Get back a draft requirement.md in the ADLC toolkit's template, and a list of the questions your notes leave unanswered, each marked blocking or assumption.

Before you run it

  • Your text goes from this page straight to Anthropic's API, under your own API key. You pay for the call. Nothing goes through a server of ours.
  • Nothing is stored. Not your key, not your text, not the result: no logs, no browser storage, no analytics. Reloading the page forgets everything.
  • Some common credential formats (API keys, access tokens) are removed from your text before it is sent. Other secrets are not, so check what you paste.
  • Consider a separate key with a low spend limit, and revoke it when you are done.
  • Do not paste anything you may not share with Anthropic.
